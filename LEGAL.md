5.1")**, which Wizards of the Coast released under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International License (CC-BY-4.0). We do **not** use non-SRD

creatures, settings, characters, or named IP.

ability-modifier and proficiency-bonus formulas, ability checks / skill checks /
saving throws, attack and damage resolution (including critical hits), and the
SRD damage types with resistance / immunity / vulnerability rules.
